How it works

From one sentence to a story, with you in charge.

Edupilo does the scriptwriting, illustration and animation. You make the educational decisions: what to teach, how it’s explained, and whether it’s right.

For parents & teachers

Made for the adult in charge.

AI drafts the lesson. You stay the expert on the child in front of you, and nothing reaches them without your approval.

For parents

When the textbook explanation isn’t landing.

Explain it the way your child already thinks: a favourite animal, a snack, a game. You get a short story you’ve checked yourself.

  • You approve every scene before anything is animated.
  • Counts and equations are calculated, never guessed by an image generator.
  • Adults hold the account. Children never need one.

For teachers

A visual explanation for the whole class.

Turn this week’s topic into a short story pitched at your class level, ready to play on the classroom screen.

  • Vocabulary, pacing and captions match the class, from Nursery to Class 4.
  • Large, uncluttered labels designed to read from the back of the room.
  • Not tied to any one board. You decide how it fits your teaching.

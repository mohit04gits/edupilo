# Decision Register

## AI Educational Story Video Platform — V1

**Source of truth:** `docs/PRD.md`  
**PRD status:** Active Draft — Core Product and Visual Direction Confirmed; Remaining Operational Decisions Open  
**Register purpose:** Track documented decisions without changing, interpreting away, or silently resolving PRD approval states.

## Status interpretation

| Register category | Meaning in this register |
|---|---|
| Approved | Explicit founder approval is recorded, a checklist item is checked, or the PRD explicitly labels the item an approved direction/default. |
| Proposed | Recommended or presented as a candidate, but not approved. |
| Pending Founder Approval | The PRD requires founder confirmation or leaves the checklist item open. |
| Deferred | Explicitly outside V1 or postponed until evidence justifies reconsideration. |
| Rejected/Superseded | A previous direction was replaced or removed from the current product direction. |

“Agreed direction” is preserved in the detailed status column. It means the requirement is included in the supplied product direction, but it does not override an unchecked founder-approval item elsewhere in the PRD.
